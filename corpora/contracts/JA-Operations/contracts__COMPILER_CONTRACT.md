# JA Operations Compiler Contract

A conforming compiler should:

1. Parse the provisional surface without hidden network or host execution.
2. Produce stable source spans and typed AST nodes.
3. Resolve workspaces, targets, profiles, services, workers, bindings, probes, transitions, and artifacts.
4. Infer effects and required capabilities.
5. Evaluate network, secret, build, deployment, package, and ledger policy.
6. Reject implicit authority and incomplete recovery topology.
7. Lower accepted nodes to stable R12 records.
8. Preserve diagnostic and provenance identities across safe optimization.
9. Emit a deterministic, target-neutral plan or a precise unsupported-feature diagnostic.
10. Clearly label modeled, structural, and native evidence.
