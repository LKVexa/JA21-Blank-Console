# QUORUM RC-PW 7.0.0 — Generations, Lineage, and Succession

Support long-lived worlds where individual entities and institutional role occupants change over time.

Model:
- birth/creation;
- parent/creator lineage;
- age/lifecycle;
- household/group succession;
- inheritance;
- ownership transfer;
- occupational succession;
- institutional office succession;
- retirement/death/destruction;
- memorial/historical reference.

Roles and identities are distinct. A role may persist while occupants change; an identity may never be recycled.

Qualification must verify multi-generation save/replay, archival compaction, inheritance, role succession, and historical queries.
