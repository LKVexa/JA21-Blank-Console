Version 6.0.0 advances RC-PW from an operational folded-world runtime/world-compiler qualification series into a **Sovereign World Fabric** program: the same canonical world may execute in one process or across multiple deterministic worker/partition layouts without changing its authoritative meaning.

## Governing invariant

**Execution layout may change; canonical world meaning may not.**

Presentation may fold, simulation fidelity may collapse, work may migrate between workers, and assets may be evicted, while canonical identity, topology, time, route progress, ownership, causality, and unresolved obligations remain authoritative.
