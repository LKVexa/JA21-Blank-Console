# QUORUM RC-PW Prompt & Workflow Series 7.0.0 — Release Notes

## Upgrade objective
Version 7.0.0 advances RC-PW from a Sovereign World Fabric into an **Autonomous World Continuum**: a folded world that can expand, age, generate new canonical regions, evolve settlements/institutions/ecology, model generations and information locality, archive distant history, and later rehydrate it without rewriting established canonical truth.

- 20 preserved architectural work packages.
- 482 inherited atomic requirements.
- 100 new 7.0 requirements.
- **582 total atomic requirements.**
- **80 Golden World scenarios.**
- QP0–QP4 execution profiles retained.
- WQ0–WQ4 world-quality profiles retained.
- Fabric worker/conformance profiles retained.
- Master workflow expanded to **Q0–Q36**.

## Governing invariant
**The world may grow and age, but established canonical history does not silently change.**

Presentation may fold, fidelity may collapse, execution may repartition, and historical detail may archive, while canonical identity, topology, time, ownership, causal obligations, provenance, and historical proof boundaries remain authoritative.
