# QUORUM RC-PW 7.0.0 — Transport, Mounts, and Moving Frames

Model mounts, wagons/vehicles, trains, elevators, ships, moving platforms, and comparable transport as canonical entities with route/phase, velocity, passengers/cargo, ownership, damage, and local moving-frame semantics.

A transport entity may become a temporary reference/authority well.

Passenger and cargo identities remain canonical; boarding/disembarking are transactional ownership/topology events. Folded presentation may compress the surrounding world while canonical route progress and arrival time remain unchanged.
