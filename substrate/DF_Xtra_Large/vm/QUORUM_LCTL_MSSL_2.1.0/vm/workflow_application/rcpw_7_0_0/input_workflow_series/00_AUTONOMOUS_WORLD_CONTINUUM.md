# QUORUM RC-PW 7.0.0 — Autonomous World Continuum

## Mission
Evolve the RC-PW world from a persistent runtime into a long-lived autonomous continuum that can expand, age, archive, rehydrate, and develop independently of immediate player presence.

## Governing principles
- established canonical history is append-only except through explicit migration/retcon events;
- world expansion adds canonical truth rather than overwriting prior canonical truth;
- autonomous processes operate under deterministic or evidence-recorded rules;
- simulation fidelity may collapse, but unresolved obligations and historical proof boundaries remain;
- generated content carries provenance equal in rigor to authored content;
- NPC/institution knowledge is local and fallible, while canonical truth remains authoritative;
- generational succession does not recycle identity;
- archival compaction declares information loss honestly.

## Continuum domains
- world expansion/frontier synthesis;
- demographic generations;
- settlements/infrastructure;
- institutions/law/culture;
- ecology/climate succession;
- trade/economy;
- knowledge/rumor/information;
- emergent events;
- world eras/history;
- archive/rehydration.
